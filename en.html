Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Dinayet Ezan Vakti app (the "App") handles user data. The App is developed by Yiğit Can Dağıstan and offered through the App Store; the same person is the data controller for your personal data (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, "KVKK", Art. 3(1)(ı)). Contact: support@kervandeveloper.com. We respect your privacy and take it seriously.

Summary

The App does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect or store your personal information. Your records, such as prayer tracking, the dhikr counter, the missed-prayer (qada) log and your preferences, are stored only on your own device and are never added to any network request. For prayer times, weather and nearby mosques, a rounded version of your location is sent in real time to the third-party services listed below. In some countries outside Türkiye, the name of the city you are in is sent to Kervan’s server to obtain prayer times from Diyanet (details below). The App currently contains no advertising and does not use the microphone.

Information We Do and Do Not Collect

Information we do not collect: The App does not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number or similar personal identification details. There is no user account system.

Information stored on your device: Your prayer tracking records, missed-prayer counters, dhikr counters, streaks and app preferences (such as your selected city and easy mode) are kept only in your device’s local storage. If you delete the App, this data is also deleted from the device.

Device backup: If iCloud Backup (iOS) or Google backup (Android) is turned on, the operating system may include the App’s data in the backup stored in your own Apple or Google account. That backup is in your account; the developer has no access to it.

Microphone: The App does not use the microphone and does not ask for microphone permission.

Location Information

We may need your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction accurately.

  • Automatic location: If you grant permission, we use your device’s location to show prayer times, the Qibla direction, the weather and nearby mosques. Your location is stored on your device; the Qibla direction is calculated on your device. For prayer-time and weather requests, coordinates are rounded to about 1 km, and for nearby-mosque requests to about 100 m, and are sent in real time only to the services listed in the "Third-Party Services" section. Your coordinates are not sent to us. In some countries outside Türkiye, the name of the city you are in (and, if no match is found for that name, the neighbourhood/district or region name provided by your device) is sent together with the country to Kervan’s server (Cloudflare; server location: Cloudflare’s global network) to obtain prayer times from Diyanet; the monthly prayer times are then requested with the matching Diyanet district number. No coordinates, device identifier or account information are added to these requests; the server does not store them linked to a person and keeps only a city-level prayer-time cache. Your device’s operating-system service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) may be used to display the name of the city you are in.
  • Manual city selection: If you prefer not to grant location permission, you can choose your city from the list yourself. In that case, the centre coordinates of the city you selected are sent to the services, not your personal location.

Location permission is entirely optional, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

To show up-to-date information, the App connects to the following free third-party services. The information sent to each one is listed next to it:

  • Aladhan API (prayer times, monthly timetable, Hijri calendar and date converter): location coordinates rounded to about 1 km and the date; for the date converter, only the date you enter.
  • Open-Meteo (weather): location coordinates rounded to about 1 km.
  • Cloudflare (infrastructure of Kervan’s prayer-time server; prayer times and the monthly timetable in some countries outside Türkiye): the city name (if needed, the neighbourhood/district or region name), the country and the Diyanet district number; no coordinates are sent.
  • Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet) Awqat Salah API (source of prayer times in some countries outside Türkiye): the App does not connect to it directly; Kervan’s server fetches the times using the district number, and no information about you is passed to Diyanet.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (nearby mosques): location coordinates rounded to about 100 m and a 3 km search radius.
  • Al Quran Cloud API (Quran text, verse of the day and audio recitation): no personal data is sent; only the surah/verse number.
  • fawazahmed0/hadith-api via the jsDelivr CDN (hadith of the day): no personal data is sent.
  • mp3quran.net (Quran radio stations and streams): no personal data is sent.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (optional support purchase): payment runs entirely through the store’s infrastructure; details are in the "In-App Purchases" section below.

As with any internet request, your device’s IP address is technically visible to the service it connects to. The App does not add your name, e-mail address, device identifier or worship records to these requests. These services’ own privacy policies apply.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional support purchases (monthly/annual subscription and a one-time thank-you). These purchases run entirely through the App Store infrastructure.

  • Your payment details, your Apple transaction/receipt ID and information about your Apple account are never passed to us; we do not see or store them. Apple is responsible for this data, and Apple’s privacy policy applies.
  • The purchase receipt is not sent to any server of ours; the App uses no server for this purpose.
  • The only information stored on your device is a "support given" flag, kept so that the support screen is not shown again. This flag is not linked to your identity and is not sent to any server.
  • Apple provides us only with aggregated sales and download statistics that cannot be linked to a person.
