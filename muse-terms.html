2. What it does. The connector returns daily prayer times, the next prayer time and the Qibla direction for a place, through Meta's Muse assistant. It is read-only and needs no account. If no country is given, it first looks for the place in Türkiye and otherwise uses the best worldwide match, so please check that the place shown is the one you meant.

3. How the times are produced. Prayer times are calculated by us from astronomical formulas, using parameters compatible with the calculation method commonly used in Türkiye (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°, with standard minute adjustments). This is not an official timetable. Dinayet is independent and is not affiliated with any religious authority, including the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye. Times may differ by a few minutes from the timetable of your local mosque or community, and the Hijri date (Umm al-Qura calendar) may differ by a day from the calendar used where you live. For religious obligations, please confirm with your local mosque. The Qibla direction is a bearing from true north, not magnetic north.
