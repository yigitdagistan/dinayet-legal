Dinayet Prayer Times for Muse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are. The Dinayet Prayer Times connector for Muse ("the connector") is operated by Kervan LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA, which is the data controller for the connector ("we", "us"). Contact: support@kervandeveloper.com. We are not affiliated with Meta.

What the connector does. It returns daily prayer times, the next prayer time and the Qibla direction for a place you ask about, through Meta's Muse assistant. It is read-only: it does not create accounts, make purchases or change anything on your behalf.

What we receive. When Muse calls the connector, we receive only the details needed to answer that request: a city or town name (optionally with a country and a date), or a pair of coordinates for the Qibla direction. Like any internet service, our hosting provider also sees the network address of the system that sends the request, which is normally Muse's infrastructure rather than your own device. We do not receive your name, your Meta account, your contacts, your messages or your conversation with Muse.

How we use it. We use the request only to calculate and return the answer. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) before any calculation. Place names are looked up in a list of cities stored inside the connector itself; the connector does not send your request to any other company or service.

What we keep. Nothing. We do not store requests, keep a history of them, or write their content (place names, coordinates or dates) to logs. The only operational record is a line stating which tool was called, whether it succeeded and how long it took; request logging on our hosting platform is switched off. To protect the service from abuse, the network address of the calling system is used as a short-lived counter (about one minute) to enforce request limits; it is not stored with, or linked to, the content of any request.

What we never do. We do not sell or share request data, use it for advertising, build profiles, infer sensitive characteristics such as religion from it, or use it to train AI models.

Service provider and international processing. The connector runs on Cloudflare Workers, operated by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes requests for us as our hosting provider. Cloudflare operates a global network, so a request may be processed in a data centre outside your country, including in the United States. Muse is operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.; what Muse sends to the connector, and how Meta handles your conversation, are governed by Meta's own privacy policy, not this one.

Legal basis and your rights (EU, UK and Türkiye). To the extent any request data is personal data, we process it only to provide the answer you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)). Because we keep no request data, we hold nothing that we could later provide, correct or delete. You can still contact us at the address above with any question or request, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

Children. The connector is a general-audience utility. It does not ask for, or knowingly collect, personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at https://yigitdagistan.github.io/dinayet-legal/muse-privacy.html
